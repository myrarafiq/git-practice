# Software Development & EdTech Revolution Article

## Article

[How EdTech Revolution Is Reshaping the Education Industry](https://radixweb.com/blog/edtech-revolution-software-development-is-reshaping-education-industry)

## Why I Found This Interesting

I chose this article because educational technology is something I am genuinely very interested in, especially the intersection between technology and education. I like the idea that software development can do more than just make existing processes faster. It can actually change how students learn, how teachers work, and how people access education.

This is also something I have explored through my own work and professional experiences. I co-founded and worked on **Lemma Education**, an EdTech startup where we were building an AI math tutor that could listen to students explain their reasoning and give feedback based on their actual problem-solving process. I have also worked on **Izteraab Education**, which focuses on transforming Pakistani classrooms through personalized learning and AI technology, as well as interning at two other growing EdTech startups in the MENA region. Because of these experiences, I found the article's discussion of AI, personalized learning, digital classrooms, and other technologies particularly interesting.

What stood out to me is that the technology itself is only part of the solution. Good educational software has to actually understand the problems students and teachers face and make their experience better. I am especially interested in how we can use the technical skills I am developing in computer science to build education tools that are genuinely useful rather than just adding technology for the sake of it. This is an area I would definitely like to keep exploring as I continue learning about software development.
