# Software Development & EdTech Revolution Article

## Article

[How EdTech Revolution Is Reshaping the Education Industry](https://radixweb.com/blog/edtech-revolution-software-development-is-reshaping-education-industry)

## Why I Found This Article Interesting

I chose this article because EdTech is an area I am genuinely interested in, and I liked how it looked at software development as something that can actually change the learning experience rather than just move existing education online.

One line that stood out to me was: “Technology is not driving change; it is accelerating it.” I thought this was a pretty good way of describing what is happening in education. The article talks about things like personalized learning, AI and data analytics, real-time feedback, and accessibility, which are all areas where software can have a much bigger impact than just making things more convenient. I especially found the discussion around personalized learning interesting because good educational software can use information about how a student is learning to adapt the experience to them.

I've had some exposure to this space myself through co-founding an EdTech startup and interning at EdTech companies, so I've gotten to see a little bit of how the industry works from the product and data side. For example, at Lemma Education, we were working on an AI math tutor that could listen to how a student was solving a problem and give feedback on their reasoning, rather than only checking whether the final answer was correct. Reading about personalized learning and real-time feedback in the article made me think about how these ideas actually translate into products.

I don't think I've figured out exactly what I want to build in EdTech yet, but it is definitely an area I want to keep learning about. I like the combination of software development and a problem that has a real impact on people's everyday lives, and I'm hoping to keep building my technical skills while learning more about how technology can genuinely improve education.
